# Changelog

## 1.3

- In-engine cutscene of the Ring Incident (2064) before the prologue; prologue replayable from the menu and the campaign screen.
- Simpler mech editor: one tab per mech with the 3D model, parts (with damage type, DPS, range, description) and strategy side by side.
- Separate music and effects volume sliders, remembered between sessions.
- Smaller videos (1280 px) and a new trailer.

## 1.2

- Localization: English, Russian and Simplified Chinese for UI, dialogue, lore, radio and LLM talk.
- Rewritten campaign dialogue with the Ring Incident backstory and ECHO's origin.
- Radio: team talk and open-channel trash talk written by the LLM, speech bubbles tagged TEAM/ALL, technical reports hidden behind a filter.
- Briefing: live 3D preview of the configured mech, full chassis and weapon specs.
- Armor and damage types (kinetic, explosive, EM, thermal); chapter-by-chapter counters.
- Unguided splash rockets; rivals roll one of three game plans per match.
- Procedural soundtrack per arena; videos recorded with music and title cards.
- Many AI fixes: no back-turning, no stale-intel camping, range reflex, unit avoidance, walkable wrecks, explicit ambush orders respected.

## 1.0

- First playable campaign: four coaches, eight battles, LLM commander and JEV streams, Blender assets.
